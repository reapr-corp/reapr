cargo/rpr: minor (Changed)

Renamed the CLI to `rpr`: the binary and its package (`crates/rpr`, formerly `reapr-cli`). Installers are
`rpr-installer.sh` and `rpr-installer.ps1`. The product, the crates `reapr-core` and `reapr-stryker`, and the
run store in `<git dir>/reapr/runs/` keep the Reapr name.
